# Prompts de logotipo

Funcionan mejor en inglés (Midjourney, DALL·E, Flux, Ideogram).

## Principal
```
Minimalist futuristic logo for "PROMETEO", an AI voice planning assistant. Central emblem: a robotic arc-reactor heart — concentric segmented rings around a glowing circular core, subtle heart silhouette formed by the inner geometry, precise mechanical details, thin circuit lines. Glowing electric cyan (#4fd1ff) and deep blue (#1e6bff) neon light with soft bloom, on a pure black background with a few faint stars (midnight galaxy). Sleek sci-fi HUD aesthetic, symmetrical, vector-style, high contrast, clean edges, centered composition, works at small sizes. Wordmark "PROMETEO" below in a wide geometric futuristic sans-serif (Orbitron-like), letter-spaced, light cyan. --no text artifacts, no gradients clutter, no realistic humans, no extra words
```

## Icono / favicon
```
Flat vector app icon of a glowing robotic arc-reactor heart, concentric segmented rings, cyan and blue neon on black, minimal, symmetrical, bold shapes readable at 32px, no text
```

## Monocromo
```
Single-color line-art logo of a mechanical arc-reactor heart with concentric rings, thin uniform strokes, white on black, geometric, minimal, no text
```

## Animable (capas separadas)
```
Exploded layered design of a futuristic arc-reactor heart logo: separate outer ring, middle segmented ring, inner core, each on its own layer, cyan glow on black, flat vector, for animation
```

> [!tip]
> Pide la salida en SVG (Recraft o Ideogram) o vectoriza el PNG para poder animar las capas igual que el reactor de la UI.
